# AI Music Creation

A survey of AI features that have actually shipped inside music production tools. It covers the assistants built into the major workstations, stem separation both native and standalone, mastering, and the research underneath all three. It does not cover music generation models that produce finished songs, voice cloning, or artist-likeness questions. Those are a different subject with a different evidence base.

**Read the sourcing note before the report, because it is the reason this collection exists in its current form.** Consumer music software is covered heavily by search-optimized review articles, and on this subject those articles are unusually unreliable. During preparation, three claims carried by dated 2026 review pieces with named authors turned out to be false. One credited a company with a competitor's feature. One described a product that has never existed. One listed a mastering service as a current market leader more than a year after it ceased trading.

That failure is worth understanding because it is not the ordinary one. In most fields, a bad claim spreads because outlets copy each other, so a claim appearing in ten places is really one source repeated ten times. Here the articles are written independently and are wrong independently, each inventing its own numbers. Agreement between them carries no information at all. The only defense is to go to whoever ships the product.

Every statement in this collection is therefore cited to the company that ships the thing described, to the paper that published the result, or to the platform that sets the specification. Prices come from the seller's own page and were read on the date given. Where two sources genuinely disagree, the disagreement is reported rather than resolved.
