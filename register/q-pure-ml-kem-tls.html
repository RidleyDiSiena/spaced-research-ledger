Which components can negotiate a pure ML-KEM TLS group?

Any one of the pure ML-KEM key exchange groups – MLKEM512, MLKEM768 or MLKEM1024, from draft-ietf-tls-mlkem – usable in a TLS 1.3 handshake in a general release. Pure rather than hybrid: the whole key exchange rests on ML-KEM, with no classical half. That is what a pure-post-quantum mandate asks for, and it is also why IANA lists all three as Recommended N where X25519MLKEM768 is Y. The bar is available; a preview channel or build flag does not count. Added 2026-10-08, when the draft was in the RFC Editor queue.
