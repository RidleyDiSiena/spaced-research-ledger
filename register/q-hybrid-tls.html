Which components can negotiate a hybrid post-quantum TLS group?

Any one of the IANA-registered hybrid key exchange groups – X25519MLKEM768, SecP256r1MLKEM768 or SecP384r1MLKEM1024 – usable in a TLS 1.3 handshake in a general release. Hybrid rather than pure ML-KEM because hybrid is what browsers and CDNs actually deploy: the classical half means a flaw in the lattice half is not fatal. The bar is available, so a group you must configure counts here; the stricter question is the next one.
