  • ML-KEM, FIPS 140-3: Not in any module of this project: the CMVP validated list and the Modules In Process list were scanned on 2026-09-12 and name none carrying it.
  • ML-DSA, Primitive: Roadmap items only; a 2026 mailing-list thread shows users still asking.
  • ML-DSA, FIPS 140-3: Not in any module of this project: the CMVP validated list and the Modules In Process list were scanned on 2026-09-12 and name none carrying it.

Known Unknowns

Cells an engineer would ask about that no cited source has settled yet. not checked means nobody has searched. searching means fewer than 2 passes are done. none found means the search was completed and came back empty, and the artifacts examined are named; a result older than 90 days is marked for recheck.

  • slh-dsa / primitive – none found 2026-09-12: Searched twice on 2026-09-12 and found nothing: the roadmap carries ML-DSA items and puts ML-KEM in an unscheduled Future bucket, and SLH-DSA appears in neither. The maintainers’ own mailing-list answer is that no PQC timeline is committed, that the PSA Crypto API for it is still being defined, and that after TF-PSA-Crypto 1.0 and Mbed TLS 4.0 the work will compete with other features. Absent by plan rather than by oversight. Examined: Mbed TLS published roadmap, TrustedFirmware mbed-tls mailing list thread on quantum-safe algorithms.
