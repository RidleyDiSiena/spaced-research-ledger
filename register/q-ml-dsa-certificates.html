ML-DSA in certificates and keys – generating, parsing, signing, verifying or chain-building – in a general release. The bar is available. This is the question that decides whether a post-quantum PKI is deployable at all, and it is deliberately separate from the TLS questions: a stack can negotiate a hybrid key exchange while being unable to validate a post-quantum certificate, and most can.
