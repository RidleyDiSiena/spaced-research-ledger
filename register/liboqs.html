Notes

  • ML-KEM, Primitive: mlkem-native is the default implementation; updated to v1.2.0 in 0.16.0.
  • SLH-DSA, Primitive: 0.15.0 was the last release with pre-standard SPHINCS+; SLH-DSA supersedes it and SPHINCS+ was removed in 0.16.0.
  • XMSS / XMSS^MT, Primitive: 0.16.0 fixed an out-of-bounds read in XMSS/XMSS^MT verification.
