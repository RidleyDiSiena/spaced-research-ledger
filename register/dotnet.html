Notes

  • ML-KEM, Primitive: Requires Windows with CNG PQC support or an OpenSSL with the algorithms.
  • ML-DSA, Primitive: MLDsa members renamed SecretKey* to PrivateKey* in .NET 10, a breaking change.
  • Composite ML-DSA, Primitive: Composite ML-DSA in System.Security.Cryptography; PEM import/export helpers still experimental behind SYSLIB5006 (d0001:20).
