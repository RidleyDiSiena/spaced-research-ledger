Notes

  • ML-KEM, Primitive: ML-KEM-768/1024 behind the TLS hybrid from 1.24; crypto/hpke with the X-Wing option from 1.26.
  • ML-KEM, TLS 1.3: Pure MLKEM1024 key exchange.
  • ML-KEM, FIPS 140-3: Inside Go Cryptographic Module, Geomys LLC, FIPS 140-3 certificate 5247 issued 2026-04-27. Sunset 2031-04-26.
  • X25519MLKEM768, TLS 1.3: On when CurvePreferences is nil; GODEBUG tlsmlkem=0 reverts.
  • X25519MLKEM768, FIPS 140-3: Listed as FIPS-allowed but unreachable under GODEBUG=fips140=only per an open March 2026 issue.
  • ML-DSA, Primitive: crypto/mldsa with MLDSA44/65/87.
  • ML-DSA, TLS 1.3: ML-DSA signature schemes for TLS 1.3.

Known Unknowns

Cells an engineer would ask about that no cited source has settled yet. not checked means nobody has searched. searching means fewer than 2 passes are done. none found means the search was completed and came back empty, and the artifacts examined are named; a result older than 90 days is marked for recheck.

  • slh-dsa / primitive – none found 2026-09-12: Searched twice on 2026-09-12 and found nothing: SLH-DSA appears nowhere in the Go 1.27 notes, which introduce crypto/mldsa and name every other post-quantum addition explicitly. Go has shipped ML-KEM and ML-DSA as their standards landed; the silence on SLH-DSA is meaningful rather than incidental. Examined: Go 1.27 release notes, Go 1.26 release notes (crypto/hpke, ML-KEM).
