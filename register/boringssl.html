Notes

  • ML-KEM, TLS 1.3: A pure ML-KEM group is supported: kNamedGroups carries MLKEM1024 (SSL_GROUP_MLKEM1024) alongside the X25519MLKEM768 hybrid. MLKEM768 and MLKEM512 are not in the array.
  • ML-KEM, FIPS 140-3: Not Google’s own validation: BoringSSL has never pursued outside FIPS validation. The only ML-KEM path is SafeLogic’s CryptoComply BoringCrypto, a third-party packaging of BoringCrypto, submitted to the CMVP on 2026-05-19 and in the queue as of that date. Read as a queue position, not as a vendor capability.
  • X25519MLKEM768, TLS 1.3: Chrome shipped it by default from 131 with the standard codepoint; whether BoringSSL’s own default group list carries it is a conflict in the ledger (d0001:23).
  • SecP256r1MLKEM768, TLS 1.3: Absent from kNamedGroups, which is the exhaustive list of groups libssl knows. Recorded as ‘none’ rather than a gap because an exhaustive array makes the absence a positive finding, not an open question.
  • SecP384r1MLKEM1024, TLS 1.3: Absent from kNamedGroups, as above.
  • ML-DSA, Primitive: One source dates ML-DSA to April 2026 instead (ledger conflict d0005:1).
  • ML-DSA, TLS 1.3: Asymmetric, and the asymmetry matters: ML-DSA-44/65/87 sit at positions 1 to 3 of kSignSignatureAlgorithms, the default list for SIGNING, but appear nowhere in kVerifySignatureAlgorithms, the default list for VERIFYING. BoringSSL will therefore offer an ML-DSA signature by default and still not accept one by default, so this is ‘available’, not ‘default’.
  • ML-DSA, X.509: ML-DSA-87 certificate validation plus OIDs/NIDs for ML-DSA and ML-KEM.
