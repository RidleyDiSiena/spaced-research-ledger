Notes

  • ML-KEM, Primitive: Native implementation; the liboqs dependency was removed by August 2026.
  • ML-KEM, TLS 1.3: TLS 1.3 hybrid and pure groups (multiple SecPMLKEM and XMLKEM), shown through curl built with wolfSSL.
  • ML-KEM, FIPS 140-3: CAVP algorithm certificate A8437 covers ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA and LMS; the module validation itself is announced, not shown as issued.
  • ML-DSA, Primitive: Renamed from Dilithium in 5.9.2.
  • SLH-DSA, Primitive: Sign/verify APIs take pre-hashed digests from 5.9.2.
  • SLH-DSA, TLS 1.3: CertificateVerify and peer certificate verification in TLS 1.3 and DTLS 1.3.
  • SLH-DSA, X.509: Peer certificate verification with SLH-DSA, from the same end-to-end support claim as the TLS cell.
  • LMS / HSS, X.509: RFC 9802 LMS/XMSS in X.509 certificate and CSR generation.
