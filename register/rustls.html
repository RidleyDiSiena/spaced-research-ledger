Notes

  • ML-KEM, TLS 1.3: Hybrid ML-KEM key exchange in the core crate from 0.23.22, toggled by the prefer-post-quantum Cargo feature; earlier only in the experimental rustls-post-quantum crate.
  • ML-DSA, X.509: ML-DSA certificates enabled by default with the aws-lc-rs provider.
